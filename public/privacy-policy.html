Privacy Policy for

Application: RN Master Class  |  Developer: DnK Labs  |  Effective Date: October 13, 2026

📋 1. Overview & Commitment

Welcome to RN Master Class ("Application", "App", "we", "us", or "our"), an educational developer learning platform engineered and maintained by DnK Labs. We are committed to safeguarding your privacy and ensuring transparency regarding how data is handled.

This Privacy Policy describes our practices concerning the collection, usage, storage, and protection of information when you use the RN Master Class mobile application on Android and related web services.

Our Core Privacy Promise: We do NOT sell, rent, or trade your personal data. All data collected is strictly necessary to power your learning experience, daily streaks, quiz scores, and profile customizations.

📊 2. Data We Collect

To provide a tailored, interactive educational experience, the Application collects the following information:

A. Account Information

  • Full Name: Displayed on your user profile, dashboard greeting, and verifiable completion certificates.
  • Email Address: Used for account creation, secure authentication, password reset verification codes (OTP), and support inquiries.
  • Password: Securely stored using industry-standard irreversible bcrypt hashing with salt. We never store or view plain-text passwords.
  • Profile Customization: Professional title (e.g., "React Native Developer"), avatar theme color, and optional profile photograph.

B. Learning Progress & Analytics

  • Completed Components: Tracking your progress across 47+ hands-on React Native components and lesson modules.
  • Daily Streak: Tracking consecutive active days to encourage continuous learning momentum.
  • Screen Time Analytics: Measuring total and daily in-app study time to display your personal productivity graph.

C. Practice Quizzes & Achievements

  • Quiz History: Quiz topic, date taken, correct answers, total questions, and percentage score.
  • Achievement Badges: Unlocked milestone badges (e.g., "Quiz Starter", "Dedicated Learner", "Perfectionist").

📱 3. Device Permissions (Camera, Photos & Notifications)

RN Master Class requests explicit device permissions only when required to deliver specific user-initiated features:

📷 Camera Permission (android.permission.CAMERA)

Why it is needed: When editing your user profile, you have the option to take a live photo using your device camera to set as your profile avatar.

Privacy Guarantee: Camera access is activated only when you tap "Take Photo" in Edit Profile. The app NEVER accesses your camera in the background, never records video, and never accesses the camera without your explicit direct action.

🖼️ Photos & Media Storage (READ_MEDIA_IMAGES / READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE)

Why it is needed: Used solely when you choose "Choose from Gallery" to pick an existing image file from your device photo library as your user profile picture.

Privacy Guarantee: The Application only reads the specific image you pick through the standard system image picker. It does NOT scan, browse, or read any unrelated photos, documents, or directories on your storage.

🔔 Push Notifications (POST_NOTIFICATIONS / FCM)

Why it is needed: Used to send daily learning streak reminders, study motivation alerts, and quiz milestone achievements so you never lose your progress streak.

User Control: You can enable or disable push notifications at any time either within the App Settings or via your Android OS App Notification settings.

🛡️ 4. Security & Data Retention

We implement enterprise-grade security protocols to protect your personal information:

  • Encryption in Transit: All communications between the mobile application and our servers use Transport Layer Security (TLS 1.3 / HTTPS) to prevent eavesdropping.
  • Encrypted Passwords: Passwords are protected using salted bcrypt hash encryption before persistence.
  • Secure Cloud Infrastructure: Data is hosted on MongoDB Atlas cloud servers with strict IP whitelisting, automated backups, and encrypted database volumes.
  • Data Retention: We retain your educational data and profile as long as your account remains active. If you delete your account, your data is erased immediately.

🌐 5. Third-Party Services

The Application integrates with trusted third-party service providers that adhere to rigorous privacy standards:

  • Google Play Services: Core Android application runtime and in-app updates.
  • Firebase Cloud Messaging (FCM by Google LLC): Used solely for delivering push notifications to your device.
  • MongoDB Atlas: Encrypted cloud database infrastructure.

We do not integrate third-party advertising tracking SDKs or share user learning behaviors with marketing brokers.

🗑️ 6. Your Rights: Account & Complete Data Deletion

In accordance with Google Play Developer Policies and global data privacy standards, you have the absolute right to request the deletion of your account and all associated personal data at any time.

When your account is deleted, we permanently remove:

  • Your profile name, email, credentials, and avatar preferences.
  • Your entire learning progress across all 47+ components.
  • All quiz attempts, scores, and achievement badge records.
  • Daily streak history and screen time logs.
  • Your device push notification tokens.

How to delete your account:

  1. In the Mobile App: Open Settings > Tap "Delete Account" > Confirm deletion.
  2. Online Web Portal: Visit our dedicated deletion page at rnmasterclass.pages.dev/delete-account and enter your registered email.
  3. By Email: Send a request to dnklabs07@gmail.com with the subject "Account Deletion Request".
